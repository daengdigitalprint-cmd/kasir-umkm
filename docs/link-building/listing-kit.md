## Fitur utama (pilih 5–8 sesuai form)

- Kasir di browser dan Android, struk ke printer thermal Bluetooth
- Pembayaran tunai, QRIS, transfer, e-wallet, kartu, split bayar, bayar nanti
- Stok otomatis, multi satuan, barcode (kamera HP atau scanner USB), stok opname
- Penjualan tetap tercatat saat offline, sinkron otomatis saat internet kembali
- Laporan penjualan, laba rugi, neraca, jurnal; ekspor Excel dan PDF
- F&B: pesan lewat QR meja, layar dapur, resep & HPP, split bill, service charge
- Mode khusus: apotek (batch, kedaluwarsa, FEFO, nomor resep), laundry, barbershop (antrean & komisi), bakery (produksi & pre-order)
- Multi outlet, transfer stok, peran kasir/gudang/manajer/admin, login kasir dengan PIN
- Tanpa biaya per karyawan atau per perangkat

> Kasir UMKM by MaccaPOS is a cloud point-of-sale app for small and medium businesses in Indonesia. It runs in any phone, tablet or laptop browser and as an Android app. Record cash, QRIS and transfer payments, print receipts to Bluetooth printers, track stock automatically and read profit-and-loss reports. Dedicated modes cover restaurants (table QR ordering, kitchen display, recipe costing), pharmacies, laundries and barbershops. The Starter plan is free forever; GrowUp costs Rp99,000 per outlet per month with no per-user or per-device fees.
